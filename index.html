About Besign

Award-winning Flutter tailoring platform. I co-developed the app and Supabase backend and integrated Gemini-powered garment features.

Best Entrepreneurial Project ASU Expo FITers 26 2nd Place NTP13 2026
Problem
Getting clothes made runs on guesswork. Customers cannot picture the result before committing, measurements are taken by hand and vary from tailor to tailor, and there is no way to check what arrived against what was promised.
